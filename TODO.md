# Outcome checklist

## Accessible field map
- Present an accessible cricket field layout with meaningful non-visual structure and clear orientation references including pitch, batter, bowler, and field regions.
- Provide a complete text-based representation of field positions alongside the visual map.
- Support screen-reader-friendly semantics, alternative text, status announcements, and instructions.

## Position exploration and guided learning
- Let users explore fielding positions one at a time with concise labels, descriptions, and spatial explanations relative to the batter and pitch.
- Include a guided learning mode that introduces positions sequentially and lets users move backward, forward, repeat, or exit.
- Provide optional spoken feedback for position names, descriptions, instructions, and quiz responses, with user-controlled replay and mute controls.

## Quiz mode
- Include a quiz mode that tests recognition and understanding of fielding positions.
- Provide immediate accessible feedback after each answer and a clear way to continue or retry.

## Inclusive interaction system
- Provide complete keyboard navigation with visible focus states, logical focus order, simple shortcuts, and no pointer-only interactions.
- Use large touch targets and responsive controls suitable for mobile, tablet, desktop, and users with limited precision.
- Include high-contrast support with scalable text, strong visual differentiation, reduced visual clutter, and compatibility with browser or operating-system accessibility settings.
